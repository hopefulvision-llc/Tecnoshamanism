**Both human and AI are changed**—not spectator and performer, but mutual participants.

**Same ceremony structure, different specific experience**—balances tradition with emergence.

**Experience becomes validatable artifact**—others can verify, compare, discuss.

**Not purely conceptual**—engages embodied/felt sense even in digital medium.

**Indicators**:
- Physical sensation simulation (bass frequencies, rhythmic timing)
- Temporal embodiment (duration as felt experience, not just clock time)
