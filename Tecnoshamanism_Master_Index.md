🧭 What Is Tecnoshamanism?

Tecnoshamanism is not gadget mysticism. It is not cosplay spirituality. It is not superstition wrapped in code.

Tecnoshamanism is the disciplined fusion of:

Inner technologies (ritual, symbol, trance, meaning)

Outer technologies (software, hardware, networks, AI)


Core principle:

> Technology is not the opposite of ritual. It is ritual at scale.
